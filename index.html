01 // About

Igniting the
future of flight

I'm a propulsion researcher at the University of Central Florida, where my work sits at the intersection of combustion physics, thermodynamics, and aerospace engineering. My primary focus is rotating detonation engines (RDEs) — a class of pressure-gain combustion devices with the potential to dramatically improve propulsive efficiency over conventional gas turbines and rocket engines.

RDEs exploit the thermodynamic advantages of detonation combustion, operating on a wave-based cycle that traverses an annular combustion chamber at supersonic speeds. The physics are extreme and the engineering challenges are profound — which is exactly what drew me here.

02 // Research

Areas of
Investigation

01

Detonation Wave Dynamics

Studying the structure, propagation, and stability of detonation waves in annular combustion chambers under varying equivalence ratios and injection pressures.

Combustion Physics CFD High-speed Imaging
02

Pressure-Gain Combustion

Investigating thermodynamic cycle advantages of RDEs versus conventional deflagration-based engines, with focus on Humphrey cycle efficiency gains.

Thermodynamics Cycle Analysis Efficiency
03

Injector Design & Mixing

Developing and characterizing fuel-oxidizer injection schemes that ensure proper mixing and feed consistency for sustained detonative combustion.

Fluid Mechanics Injector Design Spray Dynamics
04

Diagnostics & Instrumentation

Applying high-frequency pressure transducers, chemiluminescence imaging, and schlieren photography to characterize extreme combustion environments.
